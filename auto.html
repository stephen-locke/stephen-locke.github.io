Option Price Down payment Loan amount Term Rate (APR) Monthly payment Total interest Finance charges Total finance cost Effective APR Total cost

Payments assume a fixed-rate, fully amortizing loan with monthly compounding. Finance charges rolled into the loan are added to the loan amount and accrue interest; charges paid up front are due at signing. Total finance cost = interest + finance charges. Effective APR is the rate that accounts for the finance charges, similar to the APR on a lender's disclosure. Total cost = down payment + up-front charges + all monthly payments. Figures exclude taxes, title, registration and other fees unless you include them in the price. Lowest values in each column are highlighted in green.
